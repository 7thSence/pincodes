❄️ AC PMS Update :: PBG Voice & Email — Dealer Purchase

PMS Call for PBG AC Product — Key guidelines for all advisors.

📋 PMS Policy — Warranty
  • 1️⃣ Within warranty — AC has 1 year warranty. IW customers who purchased from a local dealer get 2 free PMS services:
         • 1st — DRY AC Service
         • 2nd — JET AC Service
  • 2️⃣ Do NOT inform about JET AC service proactively. If the customer asks upfront, advisor should register the JET AC service call
  • 3️⃣ Check AC Object ID & warranty — if IW and customer has not previously taken JET AC service, 1 JET AC service will be free within product warranty
  • 4️⃣ Upon registering JET AC service call — if customer/dealer asks, inform about the free service
🚫 Important Restrictions
  • 5️⃣ We do NOT have WET AC service — do not register any call under WET AC service for PBG AC products
💰 Out of Warranty Charges
  • 6️⃣ If product is OW (Out of Warranty), AC PMS service is chargeable. Inform charges before registering:
         • DRY AC Service — ₹249/- per service
         • JET AC Service — ₹599/- per service
✅ Advisor Checklist
  • 7️⃣ Confirm date of purchase for AC product and validate the date in system after registering PMS call
  • 8️⃣ Check in Service Order for PMS service history: Search >> Service Order — inform customer accordingly
